One Step helps you begin when a goal feels too big.

Type a short goal such as "exercise" or "reading". The app suggests a few small actions you can do right now, then helps you check in and adjust the difficulty.

Features
* Instant suggestions from an AI model you configure
* Learning paths built from your own Markdown materials
* Self check-ins with attempts, minutes, and difficulty
* Progress summaries for the last two weeks
* Encrypted backup and restore
* Direct P2P sync between the Windows app and Android on the same Wi-Fi

Privacy
* No account and no analytics
* Records are stored only on your device
* API keys are kept in Android encrypted storage
* AI requests go only to the endpoint you configure
